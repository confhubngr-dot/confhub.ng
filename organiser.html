Your dashboards

Saved on this device.

There is no password

Each member of the organising committee gets a personal link by email. Opening that link is your sign in — nothing to remember, nothing to create.

  • Look for the email "Your organiser dashboard" from your conference organisers.
  • Tap the link. It opens your dashboard straight away.
  • Save it — add it to your home screen or bookmark it. It keeps working all year.

Have your link?

Paste it here and we'll open it and remember it on this device.

Lost your link? Ask your LOC chairman or secretariat to resend it — they can do that in one click. For anything else, email hello@confhub.ng.
