Registration desk

Four hundred delegates. No paper list.

Every delegate gets a pass by email before they travel. They open it once, save it to the home screen, and it stays on the phone whether or not there's signal in the hall.

  • 1They tap the link in the email. A branded pass opens with their name, institution, category and QR code. Nothing to install.
  • 2Stewards use their own phones. The scanner is a web page — open it, enter the desk passcode, start scanning. No hardware to hire.
  • 3It validates with no internet. The delegate list downloads before doors open, so every scan is instant.
  • 4Nobody is turned away. Flat battery or no email? Type the surname, tap the row, done.
